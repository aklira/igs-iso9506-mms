# C7 — MMS PDUs

## Prompt

Run task C7 of `IMPLEMENTATION.md`.

## Summary

The MMS PDUs are generated (D-013). The generator now:

- makes optional the Initiate components that the excluded CBBs `csr` and `cspi` make required;
- reads the Initiate PDUs and their details leniently (ISO 9506-2 §8.2);
- keeps as raw BER the context tags of `Data` and `TypeDescription` beyond their last
  alternative;
- gives the service CHOICEs and `Data` a `tag()` method.

`crate::mms` adds `decode`, `encode` and `validate_cbb(&MMSpdu, &Negotiated)`. `Negotiated` is
built from the Initiate exchange, one value per serving user.

**Bench**: the reference clients of libiec61850 run every service in scope against the reference
server, used as a black box. The new scenario `bench/tests/test_c7_reference_services.py` repeats
the run.

- Of the 112 distinct PDUs, 110 decode, and all of them meet the observed negotiation.
- They re-encode octet for octet, except a TRUE that the peer writes as `01`.
- The two that fail are getNameList responses with names of 33 to 39 characters, above the 32
  of ISO 9506.

**Tests**: `tests/mms_pdus.rs` has 12 tests:

- an observed request and response for each of the 9 services, the Initiate pair, InformationReport
  and a confirmed error;
- hand-written Conclude, Cancel and Reject PDUs, and `Data` with `[17]`;
- every refusal of `validate_cbb`: services, modifiers, Cancel, Conclude, the requirements of the
  tables, the 11 parameter CBBs that the check reads, service extensions and nesting.

Three expectations of the tests were wrong and the code right, all read too fast from the
ASN.1:

- the observed read response holds four results, not one;
- `0xA1` is the tag of `array`, not of `structure`;
- the `conclude` class of ServiceError is `[9]`, not `[10]`.

## Changed from task C6

The C6 note suggested the ELSE branch of `vsca` for `VariableSpecification`. The decision went
the other way: the IF branch is kept, as for every placeholder, and `validate_cbb` refuses
scattered access when `vsca` is not negotiated.

§8.1.3.16 and §8.2.1, under the standing authorization; IEC 60870-6-702 table A.19. The peer was
run as a black box; its source was not read.
