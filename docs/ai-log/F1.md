## Summary

Created the workspace after the model of `igs-iec104`: root `Cargo.toml`, `rust-toolchain.toml`,
`deny.toml`, and the crates `igs-iso9506-mms-codec`, `igs-iso9506-mms-link`, `igs-iso9506-mms` and
`xtask`, all empty.

Differences from `igs-iec104`:

- The clippy lints of rule 8 sit in `[workspace.lints]` and the three library crates inherit them.
  `xtask` does not, as in `igs-iec104`.
- `.cargo/config.toml` defines the `cargo xtask` alias that the plan uses.
- `deny.toml` allows only the licences of rule 9, with no exception and no ignored advisory.

`unsafe_code` is forbidden in the codec and the link (rule 8) and denied in the main crate.
