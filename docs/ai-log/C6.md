# C6 — MMS common types

## Prompt

Run task C6 of `IMPLEMENTATION.md`.

## Summary

The common types (`Data`, `TypeSpecification`, `TypeDescription`, `ObjectName`, `AccessResult`,
`TimeOfDay`, `FloatingPoint`) stay generated. `crate::mms` exposes them and adds what their prose
says (D-012):

- **Generator**:
  - takes the ELSE branch of the string types, as the profile has no `char` CBB;
  - gives the integers of `Data` 128 bits;
  - calls hand-written checks from the constraint check: the alphabet of Identifier, the
    reserved bits of TimeOfDay, the format of FloatingPoint.
- **BER layer**: integers on 128 bits, signed and unsigned.
- **TimeOfDay**: built from milliseconds and a day, and read back the same way.
- **FloatingPoint**: written from `f32` and `f64`, and read in any exponent width to `f32` or
  `f64` by the rules of the clause.

**Tests**: `tests/common_types.rs` has 12 tests:

- the limits of every integer type, and of the integers of `Data` up to 128 bits;
- every alternative of `Data`, the three forms of ObjectName, AccessResult and its error codes;
- a nested type description;
- the two forms of TimeOfDay;
- FloatingPoint: the IEEE forms and their extreme values, narrowing at every boundary, widths of
  1, 4, 7, 15 and 200 bits, malformed formats.

A date was wrong in a vector: day 15 286 for 7 November 2026, computed by hand. A computation gave
15 651, and the vector was fixed. A throwaway check compared the narrowing of 2 million random
`f64` with the conversion of Rust: identical, as only exact ties may differ. A noise run of
2 million mutated `Data` and `TypeSpecification` found no panic and no unstable value. Neither run
was committed.

## For task C7

`VariableSpecification` keeps the IF branch of `vsca`, which the profile excludes. Its
`scatteredAccessDescription [3]` should be the NULL of the ELSE branch, as for the strings here.
