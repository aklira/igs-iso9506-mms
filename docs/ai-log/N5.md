# N5 — Observable outbound delivery (task of igs-iec61850-mms)

## Prompt

Run task N5 of `~/Dev/igs-iec61850-mms/IMPLEMENTATION.md` in this repository.

## Summary

- The `broadcast` of the reports is replaced by a bounded queue per open association (`report_queue`, 16 by
  default). A publication (`publish_report`, or a model's poll output) takes a `MessageId` and never waits: each
  association queues the report, or refuses it (`Saturated`, `NotOpen`).
- Outcomes are events (`Delivery`): `Queued`, `Refused`, `Written` (the transport took the bytes; local only),
  `Discarded` (`AssociationEnded`, `NotSent`). They are read with `next_delivery`; those the application does
  not take are counted (`dropped_deliveries`).
- The association's delivery state, `Outgoing`, discards what it still holds on any end, through its `Drop`.
- `Server::publish` keeps its count, so the TASE.2 crate needs no change.
- Decision D-036. The plan said 1.2.0; the change goes with the 2.0.0 series of D-032 and D-033.
