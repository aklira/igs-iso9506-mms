## Summary

- **Code**: `crates/igs-iso9506-mms-link/src/upper.rs`.
  - `upper::Connection` takes `Event`s (connect, accept, refuse, release, abort, a TSDU received,
    the transport closed) and returns `Action`s (a TSDU to send, the indication of a connection
    request, connected, refused, released, aborted, release the transport, misuse).
  - It runs over the transport connection of L2: the test passes the octets between the two ends.
- **Behaviour**: CONNECT carries the CP with the ACSE and MMS contexts. The responder refuses an
  address it does not know, with the reason of the clause, and indicates the others. Accepting judges
  each context: the ACSE and MMS abstract syntaxes in the basic encoding are accepted, others get a
  provider rejection. A refusal by the user carries a CPR with user rejections.
- **Release and abort**: the orderly release is FINISH answered at once by DISCONNECT. An abort from
  the user, or a protocol error, is an ABORT SPDU; its origin is read from the user bit.
- **Known gaps**: DATA TRANSFER is refused until the data layers come (L4, L5), and the CPA results
  are passed up unchecked (L4 checks the ACSE and MMS contexts).
- **Decisions**: D-017 (the paths, the reason codes, the identifiers 1 and 3 chosen by the author).
- **Tests**: `tests/upper.rs`, 15 tests on the transport connection. Both roles, each path.
- **Gate**: `porte=1`.
