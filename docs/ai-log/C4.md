# C4 — Presentation PPDUs

## Prompt

Run task C4 of `IMPLEMENTATION.md`.

## Summary

Added `igs_iso9506_mms_codec::presentation`, coded by hand on the BER layer (D-010):

- CP with its CPC values, CPA, CPR, and ARU and ARP inside an ABORT;
- User-data in simple and full encoding, with PDV lists, presentation context definitions and
  their results, and the default context name;
- the extensibility rules of X.226 §8.5: what a CP PPDU ignores, the other PPDUs refuse.

**Tests**: `tests/presentation.rs` has 11 tests:

- the CP, CPA and data user data observed on the bench, decoded and re-encoded octet for octet;
- hand-written vectors for each PPDU and both encodings;
- the §8.5 rules;
- malformed PDVs.

As in C3, the encoder was right and three lengths computed by hand in the tests were wrong. They
were recomputed octet by octet and fixed. A throwaway run of 2 million mutated PPDUs found no panic
and no value that changes after an encode and a decode.

The module was read from the ASN.1 of task P2 (`ISO8823-PRESENTATION`) and from X.226 §8. Both are
free texts of ITU-T.
