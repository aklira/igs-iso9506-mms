# C8 — Property tests

## Prompt

Run task C8 of `IMPLEMENTATION.md`.

## Summary

- **Strategies** (D-014):
  - `xtask codegen` writes a proptest strategy for each of the 143 generated types;
  - `crate::strategy` holds the strategies of the built-in types and the checks of the prose
    (Identifier, TimeOfDay, FloatingPoint);
  - recursion is bounded by a depth;
  - the `proptest` feature brings them in, and the gate builds with all features.
- **Properties** (`tests/properties.rs`): 12 tests, 10 000 cases per property.
  - The round trips of MMS PDUs, confirmed and environment PDUs, common types and ACSE APDUs.
  - A total CBB check.
  - TPDU (with framing cut in two), SPDU, PPDU and EXTERNAL.
  - Random bytes, and mutated encodings, through every decoder.
- **Distribution**: a throwaway probe of the generated values showed:
  - the 14 PDU types and the 9 services in scope, `Unsupported` included, in even shares;
  - `Data` nested up to 4 levels;
  - encodings of 24 octets on average, up to 312.

**Bugs found by the properties**: four encoders wrote values that their decoder refuses, or reads
back otherwise.

- **COTP**: a credit of 16 came back as 0, and the same for the class, the options and the TPDU
  number.
- **Session**: an empty Reason Code, and references, selectors and Reflect over their bounds.
- **Presentation**: the numbers and unnamed bits that the CPA, CPR and ARP refuse.

The encoders now apply the checks of their decoders, and a regression test covers each.

**Errors of mine along the way**:

- the first strategy of the SPDUs overflowed the stack in debug, with tuples too large and not
  boxed;
- a strategy produced pairs of SPDUs that no concatenation allows;
- a clause number was cited from memory in a comment (§8.3.5.10), then removed.

## Left open

An SPDU unit of `other` whose code the SPDU decodes itself is still written on encoding.
