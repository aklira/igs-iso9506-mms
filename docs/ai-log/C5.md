# C5 — ACSE APDUs

## Prompt

Run task C5 of `IMPLEMENTATION.md`.

## Summary

The generator now writes decoding and encoding for all 143 generated types (D-011):

- `crate::asn` is the runtime: the `Codec` trait, the tag rules, the reading of SEQUENCE
  components, and the markers of the built-in types;
- the ASN.1 reader keeps the universal tag of each string type, the kind of raw type, and the
  extension markers of constraints and enumerations;
- the generator refuses CHOICEs and SEQUENCEs whose tags are ambiguous, and writes a constructor
  that checks the constraints.

`crate::acse` exposes the generated ACSE types. It adds the abstract syntax, the protocol version
and EXTERNAL. AP titles and AE qualifiers are decoded in form 2 and kept raw in form 1.

**Tests**:

- `tests/acse.rs` has 16 tests:
  - the AARQ and AARE observed on the bench, decoded and re-encoded octet for octet;
  - every AARQ field, in both forms of the AP title and AE qualifier;
  - the rules of X.227 §7.4;
  - values beyond the extensible constraints;
  - the forms of EXTERNAL, including under an implicit tag;
  - malformed APDUs, cut at every octet.
- `tests/generated.rs` gains 6 tests: constraints, CBB bit strings of 85 bits, the MMS confirmed
  request observed on the bench, a service out of scope kept as received, and the nesting bound.
- `xtask` gains 2 unit tests.

The lengths of the hand-written vectors are computed by a helper of the test, not by hand. The
mistakes of tasks C1 to C4 did not recur.

A throwaway run of 5 million mutated and random inputs was not committed. It decoded ACSE APDUs
and MMS PDUs and found no panic and no value that changes after an encode and a decode. Nesting
stops at 64 levels, up to 100,000 levels deep, in both length forms.

## For task C7

The Initiate PDUs observed on the bench do not decode yet. The components under an `IF (csr cspi)`
directive come out as required, and the peer omits them. ISO 9506-2 §8.2 also asks the Initiate
PDUs to ignore unknown elements; the generator's `LENIENT` list can hold them. The CBB bit
strings are read at any size, until C7 settles their size.
